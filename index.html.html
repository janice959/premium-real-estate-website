Yes. Foreign nationals can legally rent residential and commercial properties in the Philippines. You will typically need a valid passport and proof of employment or visa. Duphilco is experienced in assisting expats and can guide you through the documentation requirements.

Most residential leases in Metro Manila run for 12 months minimum, though 6-month arrangements are sometimes available. Standard terms include 2 months security deposit and 1 month advance rent. Short-term furnished rentals (1–3 months) are also available in select properties.

In most cases, the brokerage fee is paid by the property owner, not the tenant. We will always be transparent about any fees before you commit. Our priority is helping you find the right property without hidden charges.

For most properties in our portfolio, move-in can happen within 3–7 business days after contract signing and deposit payment. Furnished units are typically available immediately.

Our full management package covers tenant sourcing and screening, lease preparation, rent collection, monthly owner reports, routine inspections, and 24-hour maintenance coordination. We act as a single point of contact between you and your tenants.

Who We Are

Built on Trust.

Duphilco Real Estate Inc. was founded in 2009 with one clear purpose: to provide expats, diplomats, and professionals with a reliable home in one of Asia's most dynamic cities.

What began as a small portfolio of managed condominiums in Makati has grown into a full-service real estate firm trusted by residents from over 30 countries.

Today, we manage over 250 premium units across Makati, BGC, Taguig, and Mandaluyong, and continue to expand with carefully selected properties that meet our rigorous quality standards.
